## ✨ Why Enmelo

Most players are either too basic or buried under paywalls. Enmelo is the opposite: a rare, feature-rich player that stays **fast and snappy**, looks modern, and never locks features behind a subscription.

| | |
|---|---|
| ⚡ **Fast** | Lightweight core, no laggy heavy UI |
| 🆓 **Free** | No paywall, no ads, ever |
| 🧩 **Modular** | Everything beyond the core is a plugin |
| 🏠 **Yours** | Phone library, downloads and your own server, all in one place |

---

## 🌟 What Makes It Different

Enmelo is not meant to be another basic player. These are the ideas it is built around:

- **Artists are never mixed up.** Albums with several artists, like film songs, are split properly so every artist gets credit, even when file tags are messy.
- **One library, many sources.** Your phone's music, your downloads and your server's library are organised together.
- **Music is only the start.** Podcasts, audiobooks, lofi, BGM and more live in the same app, each with its own tab.
- **Honest shuffle.** Going back goes to the song you actually heard, and nothing repeats within a round.
- **Real black.** A true black theme that lets OLED pixels turn off.
- **For nerds too.** A lossless flag for everyone, and deep file info for those who want it.

## 🚀 Version 1 Features

The first release does one thing well: **a player that runs great and feels right.**

| 1 | 🎵 **Queue** | Play next · add to end · add before/after a song · reorder · remove |

| 3 | 📚 **My Library** | Tabs for playlists, albums, artists, podcasts, audiobooks |
| 4 | 💎 **Lossless Audio** | FLAC / WAV support · lossless flag · info for nerds |
| 5 | 🖤 **Modern Minimalist UI** | Clean, uncluttered, smooth on every phone |
| 6 | 🌗 **True Black & White Themes** | Real black for OLED, not grayish |
| 7 | ❤️ **Favourites** | One tap to favourite any song |
| 8 | ⏯️ **Pick Up Where You Left** | Music, podcasts and audiobooks resume exactly where you stopped |
| 9 | 😴 **Sleep Timer** | Stop playback when you want |

### 1. Queue

The **queue** is what is playing right now.

- Play next
- Add to the end of the queue
- Add before or after a chosen song
- Reorder and remove songs

The queue is separate from playlists. You can load any playlist into the queue at any time.

### 2. Playlists

A **playlist** is a saved set of songs.

- Create, rename and delete playlists
- Add one song to several playlists
- Load a playlist into the queue

### 3. My Library

Everything you own, organised as tabs: **Playlists · Albums · Artists · Podcasts · Audiobooks**, with more tabs to come.

- **Smart artist handling:** albums with several artists are split correctly, so every artist gets credit and nobody is mixed up. Your files keep their old naming; Enmelo reads it better.
- Albums live inside their artist's page.
- The exact tab layout is still being designed.

### 4. Lossless Audio

- Plays high-quality formats like **FLAC and WAV**
- A small **lossless flag**, like Apple Music's, shows when a high-quality file is playing
- **Info for nerds** lives in the 3-dot menu: bitrate, format and other file details, in the style of MusicBee

### 5. Modern Minimalist UI

Clean, modern and uncluttered, and built to stay smooth on every phone. Speed comes first: no heavy effects that cause lag.

### 6. True Black and White Themes

- **White** and **Black** themes
- The black theme is real black, not grayish, so OLED screens actually turn their pixels off
- Custom themes and templates come later

### 7. Favourites

One tap to favourite any song.

### 8. Pick Up Where You Left

Close the app any time and come back days later. Music, podcasts and audiobooks resume from where you stopped, not from the beginning.

### 9. Sleep Timer

A normal timer in v1. A smarter version that senses sleep through a smartwatch or phone usage is planned as an experiment.

### 10. Real Randomizer

- Pressing **previous** goes back to the song that actually played before, not a new random one
- **No repeats** in one round: every song plays once before any plays again

## 🧭 Design Principles

1. **Speed over spectacle.** If a feature makes the app laggy, it gets simplified or made optional.
2. **Lightweight core, plugins for the rest.** Servers like Encore and Jellyfin are plugins, and each works a little differently.
3. **Modular by design.** Features can grow without weighing down the core.
4. **Customisable UI.** The idea is for the interface to be regenerated from XML-like template files. This is an idea, not a finished design.
5. **Same feel everywhere.** PC and TV versions are meant to be lightweight and feel like the phone app.
6. **Free and open.** No closed, paywalled or ad-supported forks.

## 🏠 Servers and Encore

Enmelo can work on its own with the music on your phone. It can also connect to a server:

- **Encore:** a self-hosted, open-source library server. Enmelo can pull your library from it, send music files to it, and use it for wishes and reports.
- **Jellyfin:** planned as a plugin.
- Other servers can come later through the plugin system.

Encore is its own project, and it is still at the idea stage.

## ❓ FAQ

**Is it really free?**
Yes. Enmelo exists to get away from paywalls.

**Can a company take it and sell it?**
No. The GPL requires forks to stay open and give credit.

**Will it work without a server?**
Yes. A server is optional.

**Is it a video player?**
No. Music videos can play, but it is a music and audio app first.

**Will it come to iPhone?**
Not decided yet.

**Why is the black theme so black?**
On OLED screens, true black switches pixels off, which looks better and saves battery.
